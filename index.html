What this training is about

Two days. Three instruments. One workflow that takes you from a raw food sample to a defensible nutrient label. You will run real samples on Kjeldahl, Soxhlet and a dietary fibre rig — not watch slides.

  • Day 1 · 12 May — Sample prep, digestion chemistry and Kjeldahl protein analysis end-to-end.
  • Day 2 AM · 13 May — Soxhlet fat extraction (Randall method), solvent recovery, gravimetric reporting.
  • Day 2 PM · 13 May — Total, soluble & insoluble dietary fibre by enzymatic-gravimetric (AOAC 991.43); reporting & certificates.

Who should attend: PG students, research scholars, food-industry QA staff, college lab technicians.

The instruments you will run

Three Borosil analytical platforms. Real samples, real workflows, AOAC-aligned protocols.

Day 1 · Protein nitrogen

Kjeldahl Magnus Distillation Unit

Smart, in-built application library for Kjeldahl Nitrogen analysis — protein in milk & dairy, food, feed, grains, cereals, oilseeds, meat and beverage matrices.

  • Touchscreen, in-built protocols
  • Auto steam generation & titration
  • Compact bench footprint

Day 2 · Fat extraction

Fat Analyser — Randall Method

Six-station automated extraction unit. Boil, rinse and recover solvent in three steps. Roughly twice the throughput of the classical Soxhlet apparatus, with comparable AOAC-grade results.

  • Six parallel sample positions
  • Automated solvent recovery
  • Reproducible ±0.1% RSD

Day 3 · Dietary fibre

Rapid Fiber Analyser

Fiber Bag Technology for crude fibre, NDF, ADF and dietary-fibre fractionation. Faster digestion cycles than the open-tube method, with cleaner gravimetric end-points.

  • Fibre Bag protocol — no filtration losses
  • AOAC 991.43 compatible workflow
  • Six-position digestion
